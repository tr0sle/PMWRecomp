This site does not include Pac-Man World Rally: you play with your own copy of the PC game.
  1. Open the folder where the game is installed (the one with PMR.exe).
  2. Right-click the folder → Send to → Compressed (zipped) folder.
  3. Choose or drop the new .zip here.
